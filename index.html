About

I’m Chef Manny, a private chef serving the San Francisco Bay Area. Modern Californian cooking is a big part of how I approach food. I cook with what’s fresh and in season, keeping things simple and full of flavor, with plenty of fresh herbs.

I’m a California native with Puerto Rican, Cuban, African American, and honorary Mexican roots, and those influences naturally find their way into my cooking. But that’s just my baseline in the kitchen. I love putting my own spin on classic dishes, learning from different cultures, and bringing new ideas into my food.

My connection to food comes from my mother. Everything we did growing up revolved around the kitchen. We always cooked together, and through food she introduced me to different cultures. She taught me that a meal is much more than what’s on the plate.

Before becoming a chef, I spent more than 15 years working in hospitality as a server at some of the best restaurants in the Bay Area. Being a server taught me how to read a room, anticipate what people need, and pay attention to the little things that can make someone’s experience feel special. I bring those same instincts into the kitchen today. I think about more than just what I’m serving. I think about the people I’m cooking for, how I want them to feel after their time at the table, and all the little details that make the experience feel personal, like remembering exactly how a client’s child likes their watermelon cut.

At the end of the day, my goal is simple. I just want the people I cook for to feel cared for, happy, and glad I was there.

How We Can Work Together

Private Dinners

A private dining experience brought fully into your home, whether it is a quiet night in or a celebration worth marking. Every detail is handled, from a thoughtful menu to a polished presentation, so the evening feels like a restaurant of your own without ever leaving the house.

Weekly Meal Prep

Consistently well made meals, prepared fresh and ready in your kitchen each week. Get your time back and eat well, without giving any thought to shopping, cooking, or cleanup.

Corporate Gatherings

A private dinner for your team or clients that feels considered rather than catered. The kind of evening that leaves a lasting impression, hosted wherever you choose to gather.

Chef for Your Stay

I will travel to your vacation home, vacation rental, or other accommodations and cook for you right there. I handle the shopping, cooking, and cleanup so you can relax and enjoy your time away.
